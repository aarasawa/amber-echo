# Overview of Neural Networks
Inputs are propogated through layers. Outputs from a layer are referred to as activation vectors.

## Activation Vectors
Notation for activation vectors is a superscript with square brackets. `\vec{a}`
```math
\vec{a}^{[i]}
```
